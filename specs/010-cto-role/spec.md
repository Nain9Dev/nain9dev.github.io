# CTO Role

Status: Implemented

## Objective

On 2026-09-25 the owner decided that the site names his role in the Contrast3D
partnership as CTO, matching his LinkedIn profile. It replaces the "Lead Software
Architect" title set by spec 009.

## Requirements

- COPY-003 (amended): When the home page is built, the About section shall
  describe the role as "CTO en Contrast3D x NainDev, la colaboración con
  Contrast3D", shall not use the former "Lead Software Architect" title and
  shall not mention a stealth startup.

## Edge cases

- The English page is generated from the catalog: the reviewed English entry
  shall read "CTO at Contrast3D x NainDev, the collaboration with Contrast3D".

## Out of scope

- The About heading "Arquitecto de Software & Especialista Backend".
- Any description of partnership products, clients or results. The rule from
  spec 009 stays: no shared clients, joint projects or results are claimed.
- The terminal commands, which do not name the role.
