# Specification: Inner pages, visual first

Status: Implemented (PRs #21 and #22 merged 2026-10-22)

## Objective

Carry the visual-first treatment of the home to the pages a visitor reaches next: the services hub, each service page, the blog index and the articles. Same rules as spec 046: no sentence changes, text disclosed rather than removed, no new dependency, same audits.

## Functional requirements (EARS)

- **IP-001 (glyphs)**: When a service renders on the hub or on its own page, it shall lead with a decorative glyph resolved from the `icon` field of its frontmatter; every published service shall resolve to a glyph (named values and the three emoji values in use).
- **IP-002 (hub)**: When the services hub renders, the introduction shall sit behind the quiet toggle (VS-001) and each card shall show glyph, title, one-line description and the "Ver detalles" action, without a forced card height.
- **IP-003 (service page)**: When a service page renders, a header shall carry the glyph and the title; body paragraphs, list items and quotes shall keep a 68 ch measure; second-level headings shall carry the green rule; the page file shall have no inline style.
- **IP-004 (blog index)**: When the blog index renders, each card shall open with a cover band whose number is generated by CSS and silent for assistive technology, the description shall sit behind the quiet toggle, and the "Leer artículo" action shall stay.
- **IP-005 (article)**: When an article renders, the page file shall have no inline style; the byline reads as a label, body blocks keep a 68 ch measure, and the share row keeps its three buttons.
- **IP-007 (technology page)**: When a technology page renders, the heading shall lead with the technology's stack mark when the stack lists it (by name or by family), eyebrow and title shall share one column, and the intro line shall sit behind the quiet toggle.
- **IP-008 (plain project cards)**: When a project card has no capture, it shall open with a numbered cover band drawn by CSS from the card's index, silent for assistive technology; the small index stays in the markup, visually hidden.
- **IP-009 (about)**: When the About section renders, the profile links shall come before the disclosed copy.
- **IP-006 (audits)**: axe reports no violation on the four page types at 1440 and 390 px; the reduced-motion probe stays clean; Lighthouse desktop accessibility 1.00 on `/servicios/`, `/blog/` and one page of each.

## Out of scope

- Editing MDX bodies (their inline styles are content, kept as they are).
- Reading time or any new visible label; new contact channels; section order on the home.

## Acceptance criteria

- `scripts/inner-pages.test.mjs` and `npm run check` pass; audits recorded in `tasks.md`; before and after captures attached to the pull request.
