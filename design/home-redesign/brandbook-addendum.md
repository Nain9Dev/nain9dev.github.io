# NainDev Brandbook — Addendum 1.1: Motion (superseded)

Status: superseded on 2026-10-26.

Addendum 1.1 (motion, proposal by David, 2026-10-14) is replaced by chapter 08, «Movimiento», of the
NainDev brandbook v2.0: [brandbook/index.html#movimiento](brandbook/index.html#movimiento).

That chapter keeps what still holds from the addendum (the three classes of motion, the quota of one
living surface per page, `?vivo=0`, the budget) and adopts ADR-0012 Decision 4: only `transform`,
`opacity`, `clip-path` and `mask` animate, every movement lasts 120–520 ms, two easings
(`--ease-out` and `--ease-std`), every animation has a reduced-motion version, and nothing depends
on hover alone. Its table 8.7 lists what changed against this addendum.
